01Architecture

A single-tower acoustic codec trained end-to-end with discriminators, supervised additionally by a frozen semantic encoder (Whisper / SenseVoice) via reconstruction alignment and cosine-similarity objectives.

Figure 1. Overview of BiMTokenizer. The acoustic codec encodes a mel-spectrogram via stacked Mamba blocks into latent zc, quantizes it through Residual Spherical Leech Quantization, and decodes through a Vocos head. During training, a frozen semantic encoder enforces alignment between the original and reconstructed speech in the semantic space (dashed paths are training-only).
